TEXTO DESCRIPCION 2: Under the direction of the Resource Coordinator - Home Care, the Home Care Attendant (HCA) functions as a member of the Home Care team and is responsible for the provision of personal care and home support services to an individual or group of home care clients as assigned. Además,  As identified by an established plan of care, the HCA provides paraprofessional services to assist frail, physically disabled, and/or mentally challenged clients of all ages to remain in their own homes as long as safely possible. Asimismo,  The incumbent exercises the appropriate level of initiative and independent judgment in determining work priorities, work methods to be employed and action to be taken on unusual matters. De igual forma,  The position functions in a manner that is consistent with the mission, vision and values; and the policies of Southern Health-Santé Sud. Además,  Previous experience working with the elderly or disabled in a community setting is preferred.
