Descripción del puesto

Identify, Design, Develop and Maintain automated test scripts, as well as support with manual functional testing tasks as required to ensure the quality of a Software product. Además, The Automated Tester automates functional test cases through open-source automation tools and enterprise tools. Asimismo, It includes generating, automating, and managing test data; the execution of automated tests; identify and document defects or discrepancies with functional requirements; collaborate with the development team to facilitate the resolution of such defects and thereby guaranteeing the quality of the software product; Supports the integration of automated tests into the continuous integration pipeline; Participates in continuous feedback with the aim of improving software development and quality processes.
