Avows is seeking an experienced, highly technical, and accomplished Senior React.js Developer with a minimum of 5 years of professional experience using React.js to join our banking and technology team onsite (WFO) in Jakarta, Indonesia, under a PKWT fixed-term contract. Asimismo, Your day-to-day responsibilities encompass developing clean, reusable React components, managing complex application state with Redux or Context API, integrating secure RESTful APIs, and optimizing frontend rendering performance.
