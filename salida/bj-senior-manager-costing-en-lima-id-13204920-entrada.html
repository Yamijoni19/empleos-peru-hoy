Descripción del puesto

The Senior Manager, Costing leads the sourcing development costing function across multiple manufacturing countries for TNF apparel, managing a $1B+ FOB portfolio. Además, This role is responsible for driving transformation to a cohesive, data-driven, and standardized costing processes. Asimismo, The leader will partner closely with sourcing development, apparel engineering, product supply enablement (PSE), brand planning, PD and design to drive margin optimization, and enable fact-based decisión making across the organization.

The Senior Manager, Costing will act as central connector across Finance, Product Supply Enablement (PSE) and Brand to establish and own end-to-end product cost governance. Además, Enable a forward-looking cost projection aligned to brand long-range plans. Asimismo, Identify margin improvement opportunities and develop execution plan.
