Descripción del puesto

Safety Coordinator - Kawasan Mall Mangga Dua Assist the Head of Building Maintenance & Repair in supervising, implementing, maintaining, and repairing safety equipment to ensure visitor safety and security. Además, Implement a daily maintenance program for fire safety equipment (hydrants, sprinklers, fire detectors, jockey pumps, health and safety facilities).

Plan and facilitate training on safety systems and the use of building facilities. Además, Conduct daily checks of the building's fire equipment. Asimismo, Monitor the availability of safety equipment in the food court área (cylinders, gas stoves, faucets, etc.).
