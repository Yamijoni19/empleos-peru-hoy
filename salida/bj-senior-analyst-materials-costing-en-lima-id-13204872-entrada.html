Descripción del puesto

As part of the VF Material team in Apparel Sourcing organization, the Senior Costing Analyst plays a critical role in driving cost transparency, efficiency, and strategic decisión-making across the Material sourcing function. Además, Reporting directly to the Director of Material Sourcing, this role is responsible for analyzing fabric cost structures, identifying cost-saving opportunities, and supporting supplier negotiations through data-driven insights. Asimismo, Bring strong analytical skills, deep understanding of textile cost components, and a collaborative mindset to support cross-functional teams and global sourcing strategies.

Analyze and validate fabric cost breakdowns from suppliers, ensuring accuracy and alignment with internal benchmarks. Además, Develop and maintain cost models for various fabric types, incorporating raw material, processing, finishing, and logistics components. Asimismo, Track and report cost trends, market fluctuations, and currency impacts affecting fabric pricing.
