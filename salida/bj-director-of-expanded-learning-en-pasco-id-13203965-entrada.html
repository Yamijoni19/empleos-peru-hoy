Descripción del puesto

Reports To: Executive Director of Student Services Classification: Classified- Exempt / 1.0 FTE(40 hours per week) Annual Salary: Grade 32$103,155 (Step 1/Year 1) - $116,101 (Step 5/Year 5)

The Director of Expanded Learning provides leadership and oversight for the planning, development, implementation, and continuous improvement of ESD 123's expanded learning programs, including 21st Century Community Learning Centers and other out-of-school and summer learning initiatives. Además, The Director is responsible for overall program operations, including recruiting, hiring, supervising, evaluating, and developing staff; overseeing program budgets and grant requirements; ensuring accurate data collection, evaluation, and reporting; monitoring program quality and outcomes; and developing and facilitating professional learning for site coordinators and other program staff. Asimismo, The Director works collaboratively with school districts, community partners, and ESD staff to ensure programming is responsive to student and family needs and aligned with grant requirements and program goals.
