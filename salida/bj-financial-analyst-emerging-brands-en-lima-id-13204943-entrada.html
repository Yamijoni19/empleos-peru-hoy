A day in the life of a Financial Analyst, – Emerging Brands (EB) at VF looks a little like this. Además, As the dedicated Product Cost Finance Partner for EB, you will support multiple brands; All packs (Eastpak, Jansport & Kipling), Smartwool and Ice breaker and Napapijri. Además, This role reports to the Director, Product Supply Finance. Asimismo, Let's break down that day-in-the-life a bit more.
