Descripción del puesto

The Student Intern is responsible for gaining exposure to career opportunities within the oil and gas technology industry. Además, This person discovers Schlumberger's role in the industry and the lifestyle associated with the job. Asimismo, Work in field jobs for a minimum of 14 days.

Follow prescribed internship guidelines for each week as closely as possible. Además, Note: While the opportunity for technically-trained professionals is large, these jobs are unique in their scope and responsibility and require a special kind of individual to succeed. Asimismo, The internship will enable the candidate and Schlumberger determine if these requirements are met before committing to a full time job.
