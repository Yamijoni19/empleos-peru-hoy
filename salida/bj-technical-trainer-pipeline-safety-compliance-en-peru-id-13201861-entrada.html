The Technical Trainer, Pipeline Safety Compliance is responsible for delivering highly specialized training programs focused on pipeline safety regulations, compliance requirements, and operational data integrity across the organization. De igual forma, This role serves as a subject matter expert (SME) in pipeline safety compliance and is responsible for ensuring employees and contractors are fully trained and proficient in Federal and state pipeline safety regulations, Company procedures and compliance standards, and use of electronic work management systems and digital data collection tools. Además, The position plays a critical role in ensuring accurate, compliant, and auditable field data collection practices that directly support regulatory compliance, risk mitigation, and system integrity. Asimismo, Delivers advanced technical training related to DOT / PHMSA pipeline safety regulations, state-specific regulatory requirements, and Company policies, procedures, and compliance expectations.

Delivers subject matter expert level training on Company electronic work management systems, mobile data collection platforms, and digital/Paper compliance forms and workflows. Además, Trains employees and contractors on accurate and complete documentation practices, data integrity standards required for regulatory compliance, and proper system usage to support traceable, verifiable, completed, and auditable records Partners with compliance, operations, and IT teams to ensure training content reflects current system functionality, Regulatory expectations, Field execution realities.
