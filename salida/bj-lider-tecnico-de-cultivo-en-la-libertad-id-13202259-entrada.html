Descripción del puesto

To ensure the optimal and consistent development of crops at the research center through the comprehensive and efficient management of agricultural operations. Además, In the irrigation and fertilization área, the objective is to plan, execute, and monitor the timely application of water and nutrients based on the crop's phenological requirements and soil analysis. Asimismo, This includes ensuring the proper operation and maintenance of irrigation systems, optimizing the use of water resources and fertilizers, supervising the assigned team, and maintaining complete traceability of activities to generate efficiency indicators related to water and nutrient use.

In the plant health área, the objective is to prevent, monitor, and control pests and diseases that may affect crop quality and productivity. Además, This is achieved through the implementation and follow-up of the annual phytosanitary management and responsible pesticide use program, the evaluation of damage thresholds, and the generation of indicators that measure the effectiveness of the strategies applied, supporting the continuous improvement of the center's plant health management practices. versee the agronomy of all crops in substrate, working with the existing research farm teams to grow the crops to best commercial practice.
