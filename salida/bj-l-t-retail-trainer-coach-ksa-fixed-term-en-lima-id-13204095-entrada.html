Descripción del puesto

Richemont, one of the world leaders in the luxury sector, has various Houses specializing in jewelry, watches and high-end accessories.Each Maison proudly embodies a tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont strives to preserve the heritage and identity specific to each of them.At the same time, we are committed to innovating and designing new products in line with the values of our Houses, through a process of permanent creativity. Además, As the Retail Trainer & Coach, KSA, you will play a key role in developing the capabilities of our boutique teams by delivering impactful learning experiences, coaching, and performance development initiatives. Asimismo, Partnering closely with Boutique Management and cross-functional stakeholders, you will bring Cartier's Learning & Transformation strategy to life, empowering teams to deliver exceptional client experiences, strengthen selling excellence, and drive commercial performance across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

With approximately 80% of your time spent in boutiques or delivering virtual coaching, you will ensure learning is effectively translated into everyday behaviours that elevate client engagement, clienteling, after-sales service, and key retail performance indicators. Además, Learning Needs Analysis & Development Planning Partner with Boutique Management, Commercial teams, and Regional Learning & Transformation to identify capability gaps and development priorities across the KSA network.
