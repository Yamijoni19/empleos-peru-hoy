Descripción del puesto

Find your rewarding career in a vibrant and diverse community within Southern Health-Santé Sud. Además, Our organization is privileged to provide care for people at every point in their lives. Asimismo, Partnering with our communities, we provide safe, accessible and sustainable people-centered health care. De igual forma, We are proud to work in collaboration with all our partners within and throughout our diverse region: seven First Nations Communities, Francophone, Métis, Mennonite and Hutterite communities as well as a growing number of immigrants from all over the world. Además, Be part of the Southern Health-Santé Sud team, who is rooted in what matters most. Asimismo, Join a region that offers a lifestyle like no other – a safe, peaceful, beautiful, fulfilling life for you and your family.

Under the direction of the Resource Coordinator - Home Care, the Home Care Attendant (HCA) functions as a member of the Home Care team and is responsible for the provision of personal care and home support services to an individual or group of home care clients as assigned. Además, As identified by an established plan of care, the HCA provides paraprofessional services to assist frail, physically disabled, and/or mentally challenged clients of all ages to remain in their own homes as long as safely possible. Asimismo, The incumbent exercises the appropriate level of initiative and independent judgment in determining work priorities, work methods to be employed and action to be taken on unusual matters. De igual forma, The position functions in a manner that is consistent with the mission, vision and values; and the policies of Southern Health-Santé Sud. Además, Previous experience working with the elderly or disabled in a community setting is preferred.
