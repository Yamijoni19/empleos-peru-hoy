Reporting Line: Senior Business Manager, HK - TNF The Área Manager, Malaysia is responsible for leading The North Face Direct-to-Consumer retail business in Malaysia, with accountability for commercial performance, operational excellence, consumer experience, and team effectiveness across the store portfolio. Además, This role acts as a key retail business leader, translating brand strategy into consistent market execution while identifying growth opportunities, improving store productivity, and building a high-performing retail organization.

The Área Manager is expected to drive sustainable revenue growth, protect profitability, and reinforce The North Face brand standards through strong leadership, commercial judgment, and disciplined execution. Además, The role requires approximately 60% of working time to be spent in retail stores, including store visits, frontline coaching, service and visual merchandising reviews, consumer observation, operational compliance checks, sales and inventory opportunity reviews, and follow-up on key commercial initiatives with store teams. Asimismo, Retail Business Leadership: Lead the Malaysia DTC store network with accountability for sales growth, profitability, store productivity, consumer experience, and operational performance.
