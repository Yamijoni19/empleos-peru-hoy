Descripción del puesto

Find your rewarding career in a vibrant and diverse community within Southern Health-Santé Sud. Además, Our organization is privileged to provide care for people at every point in their lives. Asimismo, Partnering with our communities, we provide safe, accessible and sustainable people-centered health care. De igual forma, We are proud to work in collaboration with all our partners within and throughout our diverse region: seven First Nations Communities, Francophone, Métis, Mennonite and Hutterite communities as well as a growing number of immigrants from all over the world. Además, Be part of the Southern Health-Santé Sud team, who is rooted in what matters most. Asimismo, Join a region that offers a lifestyle like no other – a safe, peaceful, beautiful, fulfilling life for you and your family.

Under the direction of the Site Coordinator or Supervisor ‐ Nutrition and Food Services, Cook 2 or Cook 1, the Dietary Aide is responsible for the completion of assigned tasks in relation to the safe preparation, serving, and distribution of nutritious meals to clients. Además, The incumbent is responsible for serving meals to the staff and visitors, performing cashier duties, following proper sanitation safety guidelines. Asimismo, This is in addition to following established standards, departmental/regional policies and procedures and government regulations and legislation. De igual forma, The incumbent will exercise the appropriate level of initiative and independent judgment in determining work priorities, work methods to be employed and action to be taken on unusual matters. Además, The position functions in a manner that is consistent with the mission, vision and values; and the policies of Southern Health‐Santé Sud. Asimismo, Six (6) months previous experience working in food handling and/or health care food services environment.
