Descripción del puesto

At VF, we strive to foster a culture of belonging based on respect, connection, openness, and authenticity. Además, So, before we get to the job details, take a minute to learn a little more about us – our values and our culture - visit VF Careers or A day in the life of a Director, Supply Chain Finance (FOB) at VF looks a little like this.

As Director, Supply Chain Finance (FOB), you will lead VF's enterprise product-cost finance agenda across the brand-aligned costing team. Además, You will partner with Brand Finance, Product Supply, Planning, Merchandising, Product Development, Sourcing and senior leadership to turn product cost, duties, tariffs and savings into clear decisions and measurable margin outcomes. Asimismo, You will set the common framework, methods and governance that enable dedicated brand finance partners to manage target FOB, cost benchmarks, savings realization and cost-to-P&L visibility consistently.
