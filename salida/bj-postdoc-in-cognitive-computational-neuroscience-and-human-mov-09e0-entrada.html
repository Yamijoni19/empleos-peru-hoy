Descripción del puesto

Postdoc in Cognitive Computational Neuroscience and Human Movement Perception The Department of Psychology invites applications for a postdoctoral position in Cognitive Computational Neuroscience and Human Movement Perception by 1 March **** or after agreement. Además, The position is a fixed-term position for 30 months/2.5 years.

The postdoc will be part of a project entitled "Mapping Experience of Action: Redefining Theories of Consciousness" funded by the Independent Research Fund Denmark. Además, The project is headed by Professor Thor Grünbaum and Associate Professor in Cognitive Psychology Mark Schram Christensen Department of Psychology, University of Copenhagen. Asimismo, This interdisciplinary project develops a novel scientific approach to the study of consciousness by investigating conscious experiences of movement.
