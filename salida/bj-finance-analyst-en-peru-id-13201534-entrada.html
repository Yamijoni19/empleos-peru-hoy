Descripción del puesto

The Finance Analyst will be responsible for executing tasks related to account analysis and ensuring the accuracy of balances reflected in the financial statements. Además, This includes managing accounting transactions and supporting informed decision-making. Asimismo, High performance in this role will be reflected in the accuracy of records, compliance with month-end close deadlines, and the maintenance of transparent, reliable relationships with suppliers, which contributes to the organization's sustainability and efficiency goals. De igual forma, This is a permanent, full-time position with a hybrid work arrangement based out of our Lima, Peru office.

Graduate or Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Administration, or a related field Tax knowledge: VAT (IGV), SPOT and Income Tax (3rd Category) Experience in account analysis and reconciliation
