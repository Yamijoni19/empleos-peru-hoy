Descripción del puesto

Bradken is a global leader in innovative wear solutions and custom-engineered products for the resources sector. Además, For our more than 3,000 people, that global reach creates opportunities to share expertise, solve meaningful challenges and grow through new experiences. Asimismo, Together, we help the resources sector sustainably advance society. De igual forma, As a bilingual Sales and Operations Execution Planner you will be a key connector across Bradken's Americas operations, bringing together our Chilca, Lima, Mont-Joli and Edmonton sites through effective Sales and Operations Execution (S&OE;). Además, You will translate Bradken's S&OP; plan into clear, actionable short-term priorities, bringing Sales, Supply Planning, Manufacturing, Logistics and Fulfilment teams together to keep demand and supply aligned. Asimismo, Using your end-to-end supply chain expertise, you will identify and resolve imbalances, manage deviations from plan and support timely, informed decisions across the network. De igual forma, Your work will help Bradken respond to customer requirements while balancing manufacturing priorities, supply constraints and cost.

Support S&OE; governance and facilitate meetings and processes that align Sales, Supply Planning, Logistics, Fulfilment and Manufacturing teams. Además, Monitor adherence to the S&OP; plan, bringing stakeholders together to understand deviations, assess impacts and agree effective actions. Asimismo, Coordinate with manufacturing teams to provide clear directions on priorities and short-term production plans.
