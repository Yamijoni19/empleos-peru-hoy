Descripción del puesto

The Investment Officer will participate in processing private sector financing projects and technical assistance, establish and maintain client relationships, and assist in managing government relations. Además, The role involves project processing, business development, and policy development within the Private Sector Financial Institutions División at ADB's Indonesia Resident Missión. Asimismo, Lead and/or participate in the processing of projects, undertaking the needed due diligence, risk analysis and mitigation work towards the structuring of loans, equity investments, guarantees or other forms of assistance.

Lead credit and financial due diligence by developing financial models and in general assessing the risk and credit profile of proposed financial institutions projects and reviewing project documents. Además, Analyze financial statements and financial projections/models. Asimismo, Perform sensitivity analysis and stress testing and make necessary recommendations.
