Descripción del puesto

Shared Health leads the planning and coordinates the integration of patient-centered clinical and preventive health services across Manitoba. Además, The organization also delivers some province-wide health services and supports centralized administrative and business functions for Manitoba health organizations. Asimismo, The Support Service Worker is responsible to provide services to patients, staff and community partners of Selkirk Mental Health Centre including a clean environment, food service, porter/laundry services, supply replenishment, equipment maintenance, transportation and security. De igual forma, Experience working in housekeeping and food service in a healthcare or directly related environment is an asset.

Experience in a housekeeping/cleaning environment. Además, Experience in a food handling environment (knowledge of food preparation and safety). Asimismo, Minimum Grade 10 education, Manitoba Standards, required.
