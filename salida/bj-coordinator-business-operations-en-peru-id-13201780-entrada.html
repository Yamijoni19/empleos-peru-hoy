Descripción del puesto

The Coordinator, Business Operations is responsible for supporting Baylor College of Medicine's (BCM) role as an accredited continuing education (CE) provider, including supporting the daily operations to ensure that quality services is provided and compliance is achieved. Además, This person is responsible for coordinating collaborative educational initiatives in a dynamic team setting using numerous formats and technologies. Asimismo, Background and competencies include planning and implementing accredited education, needs assessment, outstanding written and verbal communication skills, copy editing and proofreading skills, and strategic skills in technologies, budgeting, marketing, and organization. De igual forma, Coordinates the compliance components for certified activities to meet deadlines. Además, Supports and liaises with education partners to submit and complete continuing education applications and session documents.

Supports the development and execution of educational activities (e.g., regularly scheduled series such as grand rounds, and other events such as conferences, webinars, internet enduring activities) including registration, preparing documents, tracking attendees, evaluation, etc. Además, Develops and reviews promotional materials. Asimismo, Creates and reports results of evaluations, pre-/post-tests, and polls.
